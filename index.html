Commerce & Business Student Internship Guide
1. How to Cold-Email HR at Bangladesh Banks & MNCs

When applying or following up on LinkedIn, keep your note concise (3 short paragraphs):

Subject: Application for Internship — [Your Major, e.g. Finance & Banking] — [Your University]

Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to express my strong interest in the [Role Title] internship at [Company Name]. I am currently completing my BBA in [Your Concentration] at [Your University], where I have developed solid capabilities in financial modeling, market diagnostics, and data-driven problem solving.

Having followed [Company Name]'s recent corporate milestones, I am eager to contribute to your team's upcoming initiatives while gaining practical corporate exposure. Please find my resume attached for your review.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
[Your Name] | [Your Phone] | [Your LinkedIn Profile]
2. What Recruiters Look For in a Business CV
  • Clear Concentration: State your major up front (e.g., BBA in Finance, Accounting, Marketing, Supply Chain, or HRM).
  • Action-Oriented Coursework: Mention real class projects, case competitions, or term papers (e.g. "Constructed 3-statement financial model for BATB").
  • Quantifiable Impact: Use numbers whenever possible (e.g. "Coordinated 500+ student attendees for National Career Summit").
  • Technical Skills: Highlight Advanced Excel (VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, Pivot Tables), PowerBI, SPSS, or Canva.
3. Seasonal Intake Cycles in Bangladesh

Most Tier-1 MNCs (Unilever, BAT, Nestlé) run structured seasonal intakes (Spring: Jan-Mar, Summer: May-Jul, Fall: Sep-Nov). Commercial banks and conglomerates recruit on a rolling seasonal intake basis throughout the year.
